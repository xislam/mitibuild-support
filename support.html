MitiBuild AI — Support

MitiBuild AI is an offline bookkeeping app for business income and expenses by Mitihub LLC.

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest a feature, write to us at miti@mitihub.com — we usually reply within one business day.

Your data never leaves your device, so when reporting a problem please describe the steps that led to it; we cannot see your records.
